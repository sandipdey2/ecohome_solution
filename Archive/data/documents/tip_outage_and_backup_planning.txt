Outage and Backup Planning
==========================

Optimization stops being theoretical when the feeder blinks. A useful
energy advisor plans for the outage, not just the tariff.

Before the blink
- If a storm or load-shed window is forecast, pre-cool the house and
  top up the EV only if the car will stay home as a possible backup.
- Bring the home battery to the reserve floor plus a buffer.
- Finish wet-appliance cycles while the grid is up. A dishwasher that
  stops mid-cycle is a nuisance; a geyser that finishes is free comfort.

During an outage
- Drop HVAC first. It is the largest load and comfort lasts 30–90
  minutes if you pre-cooled.
- Keep the fridge and a few lights. Kill the pool pump and laundry.
- If you have a hybrid inverter, do not let an EV charger that is
  still "on" yank the battery dead in twenty minutes.

After restoration
- Stagger restarts. If every AC and the geyser and the EV wake at
  once you may trip the main or eat a demand spike.
- EcoHome should recommend a 10–15 minute stagger: fridge already on,
  HVAC at +1°C for 20 minutes, then geyser, then EV if solar is up.

Diesel and inverter hygiene
A portable genset that feeds the whole house including the AC is an
expensive habit. Limit backup to essentials and treat diesel kWh as
several times the grid peak rate when deciding whether a pre-cool was
worth it.
