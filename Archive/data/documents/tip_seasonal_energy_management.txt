Seasonal Energy Management
==========================

A plan that is perfect in April is wrong in July. EcoHome should change
its default strategy with the season.

Hot dry season (typically March–May in Bengaluru)
- HVAC is the dominant load. Pre-cool, shade west glass, raise set-points.
- Solar yield is excellent. Park the EV and wet appliances under noon sun.
- Dust soiling is worst. Rinse modules monthly or yield quietly drops 10%.

Southwest monsoon (typically June–September)
- Irradiance collapses on many days. Do not bet the EV on midday PV;
  default the car to overnight off-peak whenever the forecast is <300 W/m²
  through the solar window.
- Humidity, not temperature, drives discomfort. Use dry mode.
- Line-drying clothes stops working. If the dryer must run, keep it
  off-peak.
- Check that roof drainage around the array is clear. Standing water
  near DC isolators is a safety issue, not an optimization issue.

Cooler / winter-ish window (December–January)
- HVAC load drops. This is when phantom loads and the geyser become
  visible on the bill.
- Solar angles are lower. The plateau shortens; start flexible loads
  closer to 11:00 than 9:00.
- Good month for maintenance: coil clean, filter stock, inverter logs.

Festival and guest weeks
Occupancy doubles, cooking doubles, the "away" HVAC scene is useless.
Accept the bill spike; optimize the flexible loads around it rather
than fighting hospitality.

Seasonal checklist
- Update the geyser timer when mornings get colder.
- Revisit pool-pump hours when water warms and algae risk rises.
- Recalibrate any "solar following" threshold after a module clean.
