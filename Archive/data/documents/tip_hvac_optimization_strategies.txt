HVAC Optimization Strategies
============================

Air conditioning dominates summer bills in warm climates. Treat the HVAC
system as a controllable thermal battery, not an on/off switch.

Strategy 1 — Set-point ladder
Hold 26–27°C when people are home and awake. Drop only the occupied
bedrooms at night. Each extra degree of cooling costs about 6–8% more
compressor energy. Pair the higher set-point with ceiling fans so skin
comfort stays acceptable.

Strategy 2 — Pre-cool before the tariff peak
If 18:00–23:00 is on-peak, run a 1°C deeper cool between 14:00 and 17:30
while solar is still helping. Then coast. Do not slam the unit off at
18:00 and recover at 20°C later; the spike erases the saving.

Strategy 3 — Zoning and doors
Close doors to unused rooms. A 1.5-ton split trying to cool a whole
floor through open doorways short-cycles and never reaches set-point.

Strategy 4 — Envelope first
West-facing glass is a furnace after 15:00. Reflective film, external
shade and curtains beat any thermostat trick. Seal obvious gaps around
window AC sleeves.

Strategy 5 — Maintenance calendar
Wash filters every 3–4 weeks in the dusty season. Rinse the outdoor
coil before peak summer. A dirty coil is a hidden 10–15% tariff.

Strategy 6 — Don't fight humidity with temperature
On monsoon afternoons use dry / dehumidify mode rather than dropping
to 22°C. Latent load is the real discomfort; sensible cooling is the
expensive way to treat it.

When EcoHome recommends a thermostat number, it is combining this
ladder with the day's peak-rate hours and the forecast max temperature.
